# Cultured Start Reloaded

Bannerlord character creation, overhauled. Take the classic life-path start, a fully narrated life story, a full stat-and-gear editor, or vanilla unchanged, and begin as anything from a commoner to a reigning monarch.

> **Bannerlord v1.3.4 through v1.5.2. Needs [Harmony](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2006), [ButterLib](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/2018) and [MCM](https://www.nexusmods.com/mountandblade2bannerlord/mods/612). Start a new campaign to see it.**

## What It Does

- **Four routes, chosen at the start** - Vanilla Start as the game ships it, Cultured Start as you already know it, Cultured Start Revamped as a told story, and the Start Editor for full control.
- **Cultured Start, as it was** - seven chapters from the family you were born into to the age you set out at, each answer training its own skills, then you pick your start type and the chapters for it.
- **Cultured Start Revamped, a life told in thirteen scenes** - from the house you were small in to the name strangers use for you now.
  - **The story decides the numbers** - your standing and every amount behind a written choice fall out of the life you told, and the scenes settle which start you arrive at.
  - **Then choose how old you are** - move the age the story gave you younger or older before you begin; younger is less seasoned, older more.
  - **And it tells you what it decided** - a panel beside each option states exactly what it grants and any start it closes off.
  - **Your name and your clan's** - names drawn from your culture's own customs, or you type your own, and an epilogue reads the whole composed life back.
- **Eight starts, each with its own chapters, resources and consequences:**
  - **Commoner, Mercenary, Landless Vassal, Landed Vassal** - the ordinary ways up.
  - **Monarch** - found and name a kingdom with its founding story, traditions and sworn houses, or in the Start Editor take the throne of a realm already in Calradia under your own banner and colors.
  - **Outlaw, Caravan Master, Rebel Clan** - the crime that made you and the crowns that want you, a cargo and pack train and the workshops it supplies, or the houses that rose with you.
- **A full editor when you want one** - attributes, focus, skill levels and traits at once or one by one, every skill's perks on one board, and gold, influence, renown, level, troops, provisions and a start afloat.
  - **Every gear slot, your way** - a searchable armory with culture, class and tier filters, or an automatic quartermaster; battle, civilian and stealth outfits, your mount and banner.
  - **Your family and companions, decided** - how many relatives, their ages, whether they live or married away; each one's name, role, level, exact skills, focus, perks, attributes, traits and gear.
- **See it before you commit** - your battle, civilian and stealth outfits stand side by side on a 3D stage you can rotate, zoom, pan and reset, and named presets save and load a whole setup.
- **Story Mode is supported** - choose how far along the main story you already are; the campaign picks up there with the tutorial skipped.
- **It bends to your other mods** - clan tier, renown, companion and party caps are read from the running game, so an overhaul that changes them rescales this mod instead of breaking it.
- **War Sails, when you own it** - a life spent at sea can end at the tiller: a raider in shallow hulls, an admiral on a castle with its own port, a venturer whose caravan sails.
  - Without the DLC those answers are not there and nothing else changes. With it, its items join the gear pickers like the game's own and its three naval skills become editable in the editor.

## Configuration

Everything is in the MCM options screen under Cultured Start Reloaded. Editor presets are files under `Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\CulturedStartReloaded\Presets`.

| Group | What you can change |
|---|---|
| General | enable the mod, whether your life path grants skill levels or only focus and attributes, and whether modded items are offered |
| Start Types | turn any of the eight off individually; turning every one off brings them all back, since a start has to exist |
| Per start | a clan tier floor and ceiling, an equipment tier band, and gold and troop bands, plus an influence band on the four starts that begin with any |
| Start-specific | the Monarch's extra castle count, the Outlaw's starting crime rating, the Caravan Master's pack animals |
| Creation Menus | thirty-two switches over the chapters and life scenes of the guided starts, each saying what happens when it is off |

## Compatibility

- **Save games.** One flag is recorded so the start applies once; nothing else is written, and campaigns in progress are untouched.
- **Game versions.** Every download supports v1.3.4 through v1.5.2.
- **Beta branch.** One download serves the game's Stable and Beta branches alike.
- **Realm of Thrones.** A separate Cultured Start RoT build is the same mod packaged for the conversion. Enable it only with Realm of Thrones installed: the launcher does not enforce that for you.
- **With Realm of Thrones.** The conversion's own armory is offered, and its character sheet panel is left exactly as it ships.
- Only one belongs in your game at a time. Each build names the others incompatible, so the game disables whichever loads second.
- Works in both Sandbox and Story Mode.
- The gear pickers offer the game's own content by default; one setting adds items from your other mods.
- **One copy only.** A `Modules\` copy silently overrides a Workshop copy, so if you have ever installed it the other way, delete `...\Modules\Cultured Start Reloaded` first.

## Support

- Include the mod version, your Bannerlord version, your launcher's mod list in load order, and the newest `CulturedStartReloaded<date>.log` from `Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ModLogs`.
